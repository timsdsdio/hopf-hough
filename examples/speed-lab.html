Speed lab: can neighbouring pixels share work?

Runs the production display render (v6 kernel, u_fastRender = 1) and three candidate speed-ups on this GPU, at the canvas size the app draws at (window size × device pixel ratio), checks each one against the production image of the same frame, and applies the decision rules below. Nothing in the library or the app changes: the variants are built here by patching the exported shader text, and every patch is checked to apply exactly once.

  • oracle — each pixel is handed its segment index for free (read from a texture computed beforehand, outside the timed region). The upper bound on what any bracket-sharing scheme can save.
  • tile brackets — one [klo, khi] per 8×8 tile and source from the nested light spheres (ball misses / contains the tile, with a float32 margin); the pixel bisects inside it, with a checked fallback to the full search.
  • coarse-to-fine A — field at half resolution, Catmull-Rom interpolation, 8×8 tiles that the estimator flags recomputed exactly.
