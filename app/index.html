What you are looking at
Each pixel solves for every charge's retarded time on a compact history, then evaluates the Liénard–Wiechert field exactly from the interpolated history. Hatched pixels are outside the stored history (red) or where the history is too coarse for the local curvature (amber). The probe compares the GPU against an independent double-precision evaluation. Dragging the source makes the motion non-analytic; the reference then uses the recorded history.
