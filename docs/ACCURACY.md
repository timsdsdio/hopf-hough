# Accuracy

All numbers are relative L2 errors of GPU readback against the exact Liénard–Wiechert field with **no softening**, generated by `tests/gpu/tables.mjs` (raw data: [results/tables.json](../results/tables.json)). Renderer: ANGLE (Google, Vulkan 1.3.0 (SwiftShader Device (Subzero) (0x0000C0DE)), SwiftShader driver). Software rendering is IEEE float32 but slow; real GPUs should be checked with the in-app suite.

## 1. Accuracy across Lorentz factors

- *float32 query*: observers rounded to float32, compared with the exact field at the rounded point (the preprint's metric).
- *df64 query*: observers placed with double-float coordinates and compared at their exact position.

## 4. When does the causality limiter matter?

Hyperbolic motion (constant proper acceleration from rest), observers just ahead of the charge. *peak |z′|* is the largest speed of the unlimited cubic spline over the stored history; above 1 the light-cone root can be non-unique. Limiter: 2 = off, 0 = Theorem 1 (preprint), 1 = |z′|² Bernstein-coefficient certificate (default).

| proper accel. | acceleration time | M | limiter | error | worst point | flagged | peak \|z′\| (at R) | γ now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 60 | 128 | 2 | 9.8e-4% | 0.25% | 10/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.5 | 60 | 128 | 0 | 9.8e-4% | 0.25% | 10/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.5 | 60 | 128 | 1 | 9.8e-4% | 0.25% | 10/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.5 | 60 | 512 | 2 | 3.7e-5% | 8.7e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.5 | 60 | 512 | 0 | 3.7e-5% | 8.7e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.5 | 60 | 512 | 1 | 3.7e-5% | 8.7e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9969 (0.0) | 12.8 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 128 | 2 | 9.0e-5% | 0.0024% | 0/128 | 1.0573 (73.3) | 10.7 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 128 | 0 | 9.0e-5% | 0.0024% | 0/128 | 1.0573 (73.3) | 10.7 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 128 | 1 | 9.0e-5% | 0.0024% | 0/128 | 1.0573 (73.3) | 10.7 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 512 | 2 | 3.3e-5% | 3.5e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9956 (0.0) | 10.7 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 512 | 0 | 3.3e-5% | 3.5e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9956 (0.0) | 10.7 |
| 0.25 | 100 | 512 | 1 | 3.3e-5% | 3.5e-4% | 0/128 | 0.9956 (0.0) | 10.7 |

On smooth resampled histories the unlimited spline only exceeds c on the outermost, very coarse shells. A targeted run that placed 256 on-axis observers so their retarded points fall inside that segment (proper acceleration 0.15 and 0.25, M = 128, segment R ∈ [73, 220]) gave 21–73% error with the certificate, 49–96% with Theorem 1 and 68–91% with no limiter, and the validity flag marked all 256 observers in every case. So the certificate's role is a guarantee (one root per observer, so the root search cannot take a wrong branch) at no accuracy cost; it does not make an under-resolved history accurate.
